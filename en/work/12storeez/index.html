What to pack for vacation?

12 STOREEZ

How do you pack so you can walk 20,000 steps through Paris, end up on a beautiful terrace in the evening, survive a 12-hour flight, not get lost in Bali's humid climate, and be ready for mountain weather that changes in a minute?

In this episode Valeria Dergileva shares her personal travel life hacks: how to choose routes and places in a new city, what to take in your carry-on, how to prepare for shooting while traveling, which items save you in the tropics, why it's important to test hiking shoes in advance, and how accessories help change looks without overloading your bag.
