BFT

Business Fail Talks are meetups where people share stories of their failures in professional life — an alternative to business forums and conferences

The main strength of Business Fail Talks is that serious business experience can be gained not only at conferences and trainings, but also in a relaxed setting.

Listeners and speakers are completely equal; it's a format of friendly coaching.

And, of course, it's great networking!
